# Rules

1. Accountability does not imply governability.

2. Observation does not imply correction.

3. Explanation does not imply alterability.

4. A system may know what failed while being unable to change future outcomes.

5. Reverse inspection begins from consequence, not intention.

6. Forward inspection must not assume future reachability.

7. If optionality is gone, correction may become documentation only.

8. If witnesses are missing, attribution may become narrative.

9. If self-map is missing, preserved records may not produce self-reconstruction.

10. UNKNOWN → HOLD.
