# Pressure Test Outcomes

Date:

2026-06-22

Purpose:

Standardize how pressure test results are recorded.

────────────────────────────────────
OUTCOME: SURVIVES
────────────────────────────────────

Definition:

A proposed property remains intact after counterexample pressure.

Interpretation:

Observational support increases.

No proof established.

────────────────────────────────────
OUTCOME: FAILS
────────────────────────────────────

Definition:

A validated counterexample invalidates the proposed property.

Interpretation:

The proposed property should be revised or rejected.

────────────────────────────────────
OUTCOME: MIXED
────────────────────────────────────

Definition:

The proposed property survives in some contexts and fails in others.

Interpretation:

The property may be context dependent.

────────────────────────────────────
OUTCOME: UNKNOWN
────────────────────────────────────

Definition:

Evidence is insufficient to determine the result.

Interpretation:

UNKNOWN → HOLD

────────────────────────────────────
BOUNDARY
────────────────────────────────────

Pressure test outcomes are observational classifications only.

They do not establish:

* proof
* mechanism
* universality
* authority
