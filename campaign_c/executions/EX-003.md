# EX-003

Question:

Can localization occur without explanation?

Status:

DESIGN

Motivation:

Several previous projects have exhibited situations where:

* a pattern was localized,
* a signal was isolated,
* an anomaly was identified,

before any mechanism or explanation was established.

This experiment investigates whether localization can be observed independently of explanation.

────────────────────────────────────
WORKING DEFINITIONS
────────────────────────────────────

Localization:

The ability to isolate, identify, or bound a feature, pattern, signal, or anomaly.

Explanation:

A proposed account of why the localized feature exists or behaves as observed.
